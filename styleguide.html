Design system v1.0

Kora Health

A calm, clinical-but-warm interface language for New Zealand specialist practice. Deep teal carries every action; warm gold marks the things worth noticing. Status colour is a promise: green means settled, amber means waiting, red means it will fail, grey means not started.

Colour

One confident accent, one warm secondary, and a warm-neutral grey ramp. Every pairing below meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries) in both themes.

Kauri Teal — primary

600 is the default action colour. 700/800 are hover and pressed. 50/100 are soft fills behind icons and selected rows.

Kōwhai Gold — secondary highlight

Used for emphasis and ACC-related surfaces — never for destructive or “safe” meaning.

Cards

12–16px radius, a hairline border, and shadow only when something floats. Numbers are large because a practice manager reads them from across the desk.

Tables

Sticky headers, right-aligned tabular numbers, row actions that appear on hover or keyboard focus, and a tinted row when something needs fixing.

Modals, drawers and menus

Modals for decisions, drawers for building something (invoices, bookings), menus for shortcuts. All trap focus, close on Escape, and return focus where you left it.

Feedback states

Empty states teach. Skeletons hold the shape of what is coming. Toasts confirm without stealing focus.

Icons

Accessibility & principles

Clinics are used by tired people at speed, on shared machines, sometimes with a mouse in one hand and a phone in the other.

Do
  • · Put the exception first — errors, unpaid, overdue.
  • · Name the fix: “Add the injury date on the patient record”.
  • · Keep common actions reachable from anywhere (Ctrl K).
  • · Autosave, and say so quietly.
Don’t
  • · Use colour as the only signal for a status.
  • · Hide a destructive action behind an unlabelled icon.
  • · Block the screen with a spinner when a skeleton will do.
  • · Make someone re-enter what the record already knows.
Contrast

Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text and UI borders ≥ 3:1, in light and dark.

Keyboard

Every action reachable by Tab. / search, Ctrl K palette, [ sidebar, g then a letter to navigate.

Focus

A 2px accent ring with 2px offset — never removed, never invisible on a coloured surface.

Motion

110–280ms, and everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
